I am an assistant professor in the Department of Statistics at the University of Northmoor, where I work on **causal inference**: how to learn what would happen under an intervention from data in which nobody intervened. Most of my work is about instrumental variables, and in particular about what can still be said when the instruments are weak, numerous, or not quite valid.

Before Northmoor I did my PhD at Port Ellery University with Tobias Wren. I keep a [blog](@/blog/_index.md) on methods I keep having to re-derive.

I am looking for PhD students for autumn 2027. If you like both proofs and data, [write to me](mailto:mira@example.org).
